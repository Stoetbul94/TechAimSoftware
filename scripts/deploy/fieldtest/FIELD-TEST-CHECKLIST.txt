 G0. TARGET GEOMETRY  (new in M4.6 - the point of this rebuild)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Open each lane large (DISPLAYS, then click the lane or use NEXT).

 [ ] Lane 1  10 m Air Rifle   - face looks correct
 [ ] Lane 2  10 m Air Pistol  - face looks correct
 [ ] Lane 4  50 m Rifle       - face looks correct
 [ ] Lane 6  50 m Pistol      - face looks correct, and is CLEARLY NOT the same
                                face as Lane 4

 The four faces should look obviously different from each other. If the 50 m
 pistol and the 50 m rifle look like the same target, something has regressed.

 [ ] Projectile footprints look reasonable for the distance:
       10 m lanes - the holes are LARGE relative to the rings
       50 m lanes - the holes are small relative to the rings
 [ ] The last-shot highlight (red ring) is clearly a HIGHLIGHT, and is not
     mistakable for the edge of the bullet hole itself
 [ ] Where a score is a whole number, the EDGE of the hole - not its centre -
     sits on the ring line that the score names

 On the 10 m air rifle lane the holes overlap heavily and cover most of the
 rings. That is what a real air rifle card looks like; it is not a fault.

 NOTES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 In DEMO mode the shot positions and their scores now MATCH each other. They
 are correlated fixture data, generated outside the program from the official
 rulebook geometry and the target station's own scoring formula. In the M4.5
 package they did not match, and that was a demo-data fault rather than a
 display fault.

 So in this build you may judge the arithmetic as well as the layout: if a
 score and a hole disagree, that is worth reporting.

 The display has still never been checked against a REAL target. When hardware
 is available the range procedure is:
     docs/test/rms-physical-shot-registration-checklist.md
 It checks centre, right, left, above, below, and several known ring-region
 impacts. Nothing about physical shot orientation is confirmed until it is
 done.
