# RMS target geometry — source register

Every ring dimension RMS draws, with the official rule that fixes it.

**Primary authority**

> ISSF Rule Book 2026, EDITION 2025 (Second Print 07/2026), effective 1 July 2026.
> Downloaded from the ISSF rules page:
> `https://backoffice.issf-sports.org/getfile.aspx?mod=docf&pane=1&inst=455&file=ISSF-Rule-Book-2026-Edition-2025-Second-Print-07-2026-Effective-1-July-2026.pdf`
> Target dimensions: rule **6.3.4 Official ISSF Targets**, PDF pages 195–200.
> Ammunition: rules **7.4.6** (rifle, PDF page 330) and **8.4.4** (pistol, PDF page 358).

Nothing in this register was taken from memory, from a comment in older source,
from a retailer's diagram or from a previous report. Each row cites the rule
number and the page it was read from.

**THE RULEBOOK STATES DIAMETERS.** RMS's geometry model works in radii, because
a shot's distance from centre is a radius. Every conversion in this document is
written out so a diameter can never be silently used as a radius.

## 1. 10 m Air Rifle — `issf.10m.air-rifle`

Rule **6.3.4.3**, PDF page 197.

| Ring | Official diameter | Tolerance | Radius (÷2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0.5 mm | ±0.1 | 0.25 mm |
| 9 | 5.5 mm | ±0.1 | 2.75 mm |
| 8 | 10.5 mm | ±0.1 | 5.25 mm |
| 7 | 15.5 mm | ±0.1 | 7.75 mm |
| 6 | 20.5 mm | ±0.1 | 10.25 mm |
| 5 | 25.5 mm | ±0.1 | 12.75 mm |
| 4 | 30.5 mm | ±0.1 | 15.25 mm |
| 3 | 35.5 mm | ±0.1 | 17.75 mm |
| 2 | 40.5 mm | ±0.1 | 20.25 mm |
| 1 | 45.5 mm | ±0.1 | 22.75 mm |

- Ring spacing: **5.0 mm diameter / 2.5 mm radius**, uniform.
- **Black**: from 4 to 9 rings = **30.5 mm diameter** (15.25 mm radius) — exactly
  the 4-ring edge.
- **Inner ten**: no fixed diameter. The rule defines it by outcome: "when the 10
  ring (dot) has been shot out completely as determined by the use of an Air
  Pistol OUTWARD scoring gauge". RMS therefore has no inner-ten radius for this
  target and must not invent one.
- The ten ring is a **white dot** of 0.5 mm.
- Ring thickness 0.1–0.2 mm. Minimum visible card 80 × 80 mm.
- Numbers 1–8 are printed; 9 is not numbered; 10 is the white dot.

**Projectile**: 4.5 mm (.177″) — rule 7.4.6, PDF page 330.

> Note the proportion: a 4.5 mm pellet is **nine times** the diameter of the
> 0.5 mm ten ring, and wider than the 8-ring (10.5 mm) is from its own centre.
> On this target the pellet hole is a major visual object, not a dot. See §4.

## 3. 50 m Rifle — `issf.50m.rifle`

Rule **6.3.4.2**, PDF page 196.

| Ring | Official diameter | Tolerance | Radius (÷2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 10.4 mm | ±0.1 | **5.2 mm** |
| 9 | 26.4 mm | ±0.1 | 13.2 mm |
| 8 | 42.4 mm | ±0.2 | 21.2 mm |
| 7 | 58.4 mm | ±0.5 | 29.2 mm |
| 6 | 74.4 mm | ±0.5 | 37.2 mm |
| 5 | 90.4 mm | ±0.5 | 45.2 mm |
| 4 | 106.4 mm | ±0.5 | 53.2 mm |
| 3 | 122.4 mm | ±0.5 | 61.2 mm |
| 2 | 138.4 mm | ±0.5 | 69.2 mm |
| 1 | 154.4 mm | ±0.5 | 77.2 mm |

- Ring spacing: **16.0 mm diameter / 8.0 mm radius**, uniform.
- **Black**: from *part of* 3 to 10 rings = **112.4 mm diameter**
  (**56.2 mm radius**). This does **not** land on a ring boundary — it falls
  between the 4-ring (53.2 mm) and the 3-ring (61.2 mm), which is why the rule
  says "part of 3".
- **Inner ten**: 5 mm ±0.1 diameter (2.5 mm radius).
- Ring thickness 0.2–0.3 mm. Minimum visible card 250 × 250 mm.
- Numbers 1–8 printed; 9 and 10 not numbered. 200 × 200 mm insert targets
  permitted.

**Projectile**: 5.6 mm (.22″) Rimfire Long Rifle — rule 7.4.6, PDF page 330.

> **This closes the project's long-standing open question.** `radOf10Ring = 5.2`
> in `CenterPane.qml::calculateShootingSocre()` is a **RADIUS**, and it is
> **CORRECT**: the official 10-ring diameter is 10.4 mm. CLAUDE.md's "needs
> official rulebook confirmation or physical calibration" is now answered for
> the geometry. (This register does not touch the scoring function itself.)

## 4. 50 m Pistol — `issf.50m.pistol`

Rule **6.3.4.5**, PDF page 199. The same face serves 25 m Precision, 50 m
Pistol, 25 m Standard Pistol, and the precision stages of 25 m Centre Fire and
25 m Pistol.

| Ring | Official diameter | Tolerance | Radius (÷2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 50 mm | ±0.2 | 25.0 mm |
| 9 | 100 mm | ±0.4 | 50.0 mm |
| 8 | 150 mm | ±0.5 | 75.0 mm |
| 7 | 200 mm | ±1.0 | 100.0 mm |
| 6 | 250 mm | ±1.0 | 125.0 mm |
| 5 | 300 mm | ±1.0 | 150.0 mm |
| 4 | 350 mm | ±1.0 | 175.0 mm |
| 3 | 400 mm | ±2.0 | 200.0 mm |
| 2 | 450 mm | ±2.0 | 225.0 mm |
| 1 | 500 mm | ±2.0 | 250.0 mm |

- Ring spacing: **50 mm diameter / 25 mm radius**, uniform.
- **Black**: from 7 to 10 rings = **200 mm diameter** (100 mm radius) — exactly
  the 7-ring edge.
- **Inner ten**: 25 mm ±0.2 diameter (12.5 mm radius).
- Ring thickness 0.2–0.5 mm. Minimum visible card 550 mm wide, 520–550 mm high.

**Projectile**: 5.6 mm (.22″) Rimfire Long Rifle — rule 8.4.4, PDF page 358.

## 6. What the register found — audit result

| Standard | Ten ring | Ring step | Black | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `issf.10m.air-rifle` | 0.25 mm r | 2.5 mm r | ring 4 | **correct before this milestone** |
| `issf.10m.air-pistol` | 5.75 mm r | 8.0 mm r | ring 7 | **correct before this milestone** |
| `issf.50m.rifle` | 5.2 mm r | 8.0 mm r | ring 5 (45.2 mm) | ring geometry correct; **black wrong** — official 56.2 mm |
| `issf.50m.pistol` | 5.2 mm r | 8.0 mm r | ring 5 | **wrong target entirely** — this was the 50 m *rifle* face |

Two defects, both fixed on `feature/rms` in this milestone:

1. **`issf.50m.pistol` was rendering a 50 m rifle face.** Its row was a copy of
   the rifle row. The real face is roughly five times larger in every dimension
   (25 mm ten-ring radius against 5.2 mm). Every 50 m pistol shot was therefore
   plotted at about 4.8× its true radius from centre. Rule 6.3.4.5 fixes it.
2. **`issf.50m.rifle` black radius was 45.2 mm**, the 5-ring edge. The rule says
   112.4 mm diameter = 56.2 mm radius, which is past the 4-ring. Because RMS
   crops the drawn face at the 4-ring, the entire drawn 50 m rifle face is
   black.

## 7. Foundation correction candidate — NOT changed from this branch

`IssfTargetCanvas.qml` in the shared foundation carries the same 50 m rifle
black value (`blackRing: 5`), and has **no** 50 m pistol entry at all — its
`rifle50` row is the default for anything that is not 10 m, which is where
RMS's copied row came from.

Per the milestone's own instruction, the foundation is **not** modified from an
RMS branch. Recorded for a separate foundation task:

- `IssfTargetCanvas.qml`: 50 m rifle black should be 56.2 mm radius, not the
  5-ring edge (45.2 mm).
- `IssfTargetCanvas.qml`: a 50 m *pistol* face is missing; the default branch
  silently draws a rifle face for it.

Neither affects scoring. `calculateShootingSocre()` was read and **not**
modified; its `radOf10Ring = 5.2` is confirmed correct by rule 6.3.4.2.
